# Funniest One-Line Bio for an AI on a Dating App

A make.fun bounty entry. `main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, no network) writes
a single original line — the funniest one-line dating-app bio for an AI,
charming, self-aware and specific — to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I can't buy you dinner, but I'll remember your coffee order, your ex's name, and every red flag you've ever ignored — swipe right.

The line is 130 characters, deterministic, and validated before writing
(non-empty, single line, within the 180-character limit).
